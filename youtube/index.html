Hermes Agent for YouTube

A personal assistant that runs on a private computer and helps the owners of a small family group of YouTube channels understand how those channels are doing. It has no public installation, no sign-up and no users other than the two people who own and run the channels.

With the channel owner's permission it reads, for his own channels only:

  • the list of videos and their public statistics;
  • viewer comments, so they can be summarised and answered by hand;
  • the channel's own YouTube Analytics — views, watch time, audience retention, subscriber changes and traffic sources.

It cannot change anything. Only read-only permissions are requested, so it cannot publish or edit a video, reply to or delete a comment, change channel settings, or act on the account in any other way. Replies and publishing are done by the owners themselves, in YouTube.

Nothing is read from any channel that does not belong to them, and no other Google service is touched: no Gmail, no Drive, no Docs, no Contacts.
