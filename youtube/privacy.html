Privacy policy — Hermes Agent for YouTube

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who this covers

Hermes Agent for YouTube is a personal assistant running on a private computer. It is used by two people — Igor Yakovenko, who owns the YouTube channels and this application, and Vladimir Yakovenko, who runs the computer it is installed on and manages the channels. It is not offered to anyone else, there is no service to sign up for, and no other person's account is connected.

What access it uses

  • youtube.readonly — reading the owner's own channels: the videos on them, their public statistics and the comments viewers leave.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — reading those channels' own YouTube Analytics: views, watch time, audience retention, subscriber changes and traffic sources.

Both are read-only. No permission to publish, edit, reply, delete, upload or change channel settings is requested, so the application cannot perform any of those actions. No access to Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets or Contacts is requested.

Whose data is read

Only the channels the account owner has authorised, and only data YouTube already shows him in YouTube Studio. Public comments written by viewers are read as they appear on the channel's own videos; they are used to summarise what the audience is asking about and are not combined with anything else, published, or passed to anyone.

Where the data goes

Channel data is fetched on demand and kept on the owner's own computer. There is no server and no cloud storage belonging to this application, and the OAuth token is stored only on that machine, readable by its owner alone.

The assistant answers in words, using a large language model. To produce an answer, the part of the data the question needs — for example a set of figures or a batch of comments — is sent to the model provider configured on that machine and handled under that provider's terms. No other third party receives this data.

Google user data obtained through this application is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Nothing is kept beyond what is needed to answer: figures and comments are fetched when a question is asked. Access can be withdrawn at any moment by the account owner at myaccount.google.com/connections, which immediately stops all access; the stored token can be deleted from the machine at the same time.
