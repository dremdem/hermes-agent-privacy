Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It has exactly one user — its owner — and no other people have accounts, access or data in it.

With the owner's permission it works with his own Gmail and Google Calendar: it reads mail, can reply or send mail on his explicit instruction, and can mark messages as read or change their labels. It can read the calendar and create or change events. It cannot delete mail: that permission was deliberately not requested.

There is no service, no website to sign up for and no shared installation. Every action is started by the owner, in his own chat with the assistant.
